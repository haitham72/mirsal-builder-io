title: How do I read a private reply from support
question: How do I read a private reply from support?
category: accounts
tags: accounts, private, reply
screen: Help
looks_like: A private message for you appears with a masked value, Reveal, Copy and I saved it, forget it.
---
Open your Help conversation when support sends a private reply. The message is masked: Reveal shows it and Copy copies it, so keep the contents private. Notifications tell you that a private message arrived without including its contents. If this does not match what you see, send a screenshot in Help.
