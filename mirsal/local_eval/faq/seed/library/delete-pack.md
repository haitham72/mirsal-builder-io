question: 'Can I restore a deleted pack?'
category: 'library'

---
Delete pack moves it to the trash with its sticker files intact. Use Settings > Trash to restore it or explicitly delete it for good; copies that came from batches stay in those batches. If this does not match what you see, send a screenshot in Help.
