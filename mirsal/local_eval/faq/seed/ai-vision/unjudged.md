---
title: 'Why can the AI leave a sticker unjudged'
question: 'Why can the AI leave a sticker unjudged?'
category: 'ai-vision'
tags: 'ai-vision, unjudged'
---
A model answer that cannot be accepted leaves the sticker unjudged rather than pretending the review succeeded. In one local test, five of thirty answers used unsupported reasons; that is one measurement, not a guarantee about future reviews.
