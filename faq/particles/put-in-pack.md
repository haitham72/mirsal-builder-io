---
title: 'How do I add a particle burst to a pack'
question: 'How do I add a particle burst to a pack?'
category: 'particles'
tags: 'particles, put, in, pack'
screen: 'Library > Particles'
looks_like: 'The row offers Add to pack or shows In pack ✓.'
---
Render the burst, then use Add to pack on its row. In pack ✓ indicates that the burst has been added as an animated pack sticker. If this does not match what you see, send a screenshot in Help.
