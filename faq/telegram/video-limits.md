---
title: 'What limits apply to an animated Telegram sticker'
question: 'What limits apply to an animated Telegram sticker?'
category: 'telegram'
tags: 'telegram, video, limits'
---
Animated stickers use transparent WEBM video and must fit within 256 KB. Mirsal targets 512 by 512 pixels, at most three seconds and 30 frames per second, with no audio.
