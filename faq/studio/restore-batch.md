---
title: 'How do I restore a removed batch'
question: 'How do I restore a removed batch?'
category: 'studio'
tags: 'studio, restore, batch'
screen: 'Studio'
looks_like: 'Removed batches lists Restore and Remove beside each batch.'
---
Open Removed batches under Earlier batches and press Restore beside the batch. Restore brings it back; the Remove button in that list is a permanent-removal action. If this does not match what you see, send a screenshot in Help.
