title: How do I remove a private reply after saving it
question: How do I remove a private reply after saving it?
category: accounts
tags: accounts, forget, private, reply
screen: Help
looks_like: I saved it, forget it is available on the private message; afterward the message says A private message (you saved it and it was forgotten).
---
After saving the information somewhere appropriate, choose I saved it, forget it in your Help conversation. This erases the private message text from the conversation record; the screen then shows that it was forgotten. It does not erase information you already copied elsewhere. If this does not match what you see, send a screenshot in Help.
