title: 'How do I reset a forgotten password'
question: 'How do I reset a forgotten password?'
category: 'accounts'
tags: 'accounts, forgot, password'
screen: 'Sign in'
looks_like: 'Forgot password has an Ask for a new password button.'
---
Choose Forgot password, enter your account email, and press Ask for a new password. This requests a new password from support staff; it is not an automatic email reset. Help opens only after you are signed in, so if no new password reaches you, ask an admin of your office's Mirsal.
