---
title: Does a timed-out job mean the provider has stopped making my video
question: Does a timed-out job mean the provider has stopped making my video?
category: troubleshooting
tags: troubleshooting, job, timeout
---
A timeout means the app stopped waiting, and the task may still finish at Higgsfield. Ask Help to check your own job status; the existing task can be checked again without paying for a new generation. Do not treat a timeout alone as a reason to start another paid job.
