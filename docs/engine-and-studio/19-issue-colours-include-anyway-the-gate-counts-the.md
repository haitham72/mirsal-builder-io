# Issue colours, Include anyway, the gate counts, the sticker detail and Edge (`console/studio-tiles.js` and `studio-panel.js`)

<sub>Part of [engine-and-studio.md](../engine-and-studio.md).</sub>

**One colour per kind of problem, everywhere** (`CAT`, `CATOF`, `issuesOf(t, stage)`, `mark`, `chip`, `issueSvg`, `legend` in `studio-tiles.js`; the check ids come from the verifier catalogue): **orange** out of bounds (`inside_cell`, `inside_slot`, `inside_frame`, `cross_slot`, `edge_trimmed`), **purple** bad green screen (`no_spill`, `chroma_risk`, `holes`, `background_*`, `alpha_stable`), **yellow** bad loop (`loop_seam`), **pink** look or motion (`identity_kept`, `motion_present`, `single_subject`, `duplicate_cell`, `sharpness`), **blue** file or Telegram limit (`size_budget`, `codec_vp9`, fps, duration, ...), **red** dropped by the human or blocked for a reason with no colour of its own. The same colour is on the tile (border, dashed when not exported, a bar with the name, a coloured line under the title), on the numbered chip under the sheet, as a **dashed, faded box drawn in place over the cell** on both the green-screen sheet and the video sheet (and in their dialogs), and in the legend under each panel. **Hatched = not in the set; dashed / light outline = kept with a check.** A sticker with several problems shows its hardest one first (hard before soft, then bounds, key, loop, look, spec).

**Include anyway.** An animation that was made but failed only WARN-level checks (the character leaves its cell) is switched off by default (`review.anim = BLOCKED`) and kept to look at. The human can override that: the tile's **Include anyway** button, the `+` on the tile and the button in the sticker detail all call `POST .../drop {dropped: false}` -> `gates.review(anim, APPROVE)`, which is accepted only when `gates.soft_block(sticker)` (the animation exists and every failed check is WARN); the history line says `included anyway: inside_frame`. A real BLOCK (`loop_seam`, size, ...) has no animation to include and stays refused (409). Re-animating resets the decision. Test: `test_a_soft_blocked_animation_can_be_included_anyway_but_a_real_block_cannot`.

**Gate counts in the header** (the old "gate strip"): Stickers shows `N kept, M dropped, K blocked`; Animation shows `N ready, M dropped, K out of bounds (off), L failed`; every tile has the drop / bring back toggle (`gates.drop`), so the per-sticker approve / reject of the golden path is on the tile.

**Sticker detail** (the lightbox, `gmodal`): the sticker and its animation side by side, the problems in their colours, Drop / Bring back / Include anyway, *What Python checked* (problems only, or every check), *Path* (every decision from `history`, newest first) and *Measurements* (scale, key threshold, KB, CRF, loop seam against its limit, ...). Regenerate-as-1x1 is not here: it needs a live generator (Phase 2).

**Edge** (header button, `POST .../appearance {outline, erode}` for every included batch): the white outline (None, 4, 8, 12, 16 px) and the rim trim (None, 1-4 px) of `apply_edge`. The stills are redrawn from `source/plain/` (nothing is re-keyed), edited stickers keep their pixels, `st.rendered_at` goes into the image URL so the browser refetches, and every animation made with the old edge is marked `STALE` (`anim_reason: "edge changed: Animate again to apply it"`) so the next Animate makes it again. This is the answer to the dark key line: it is in the source art, and the trim removes it.

**Size slider** checked in a browser: 220 px -> 4 columns, 130 px -> 6, 420 px -> 2.
