# Library, packs, Telegram, projects, search, health

<sub>Part of [api.md](../api.md).</sub>

`GET /api/generations/{id}/export.zip` (the Studio's Download .zip: the batch's accepted stickers, animated where ready, renamed to the export contract `{emoji}-{slug}-{tag}-{sNN}-{G###}-{date}`, plus `manifest.json` v1; animations compressed small like the pack's zip below, `?light=0` for the full-size files; 409 with nothing accepted, 404 for a stranger's batch). `POST /api/packs/{id}/merge {into}` (fold a pack into another: an animated sticker upgrades its still twin of the same batch cell, which keeps its id, name, emoji and particle links; the rest moves; the emptied pack goes to the trash; the Library's **Merge into parent** on a group's folded pack; **a sub-pack is different**: `POST /api/packs/{id} {parent: <pack id>}`, sent when a pack row is dragged onto another in the Packs column, nests the pack under that one with every sticker left where it is and makes that pack its group's parent, `{parent: ""}` (**Take out** on the sub-pack's row) undoes it; `flow/groups.pack_groups` puts a sub-pack in its top parent's group, `P-<pack id>` when that parent has no batch group), `/api/library` (each pack carries `group`: its batch group's root, `G104`, or `null`; docs/engine-and-studio.md "Batch groups"), `/api/packs…` (create, rename, reorder, delete to the trash and restore (see Trash and purge below), send to Telegram, `GET /api/packs/{id}/export.zip` to download the pack as a zip: the files renamed to the export contract, animated stickers compressed small by default (`library.light_webm`: EVERY frame kept at its own frame rate (Haitham, 2026-10-14: frames are never dropped), the best quality of the crf ladder that fits ~64 KB, never past crf 46 (a detailed clip ships at crf 46, bigger, `fits` false); a very detailed sticker that cannot reach 20 KB even then ships its smallest try with `fits: false`; `?light=0` for the full-size files; every row records crf/kb/frames/fps/fits in `manifest.json` so compression vs quality can be judged; Export to collection sends these same small files; every animation also gets a grey stand-in right beside it, `{its file name}_grey.png`, a flat #BFBFBF silhouette of its still, 40% transparent, at ~2-4 KB, listed under the manifest's `grey`, never sent to the collection: docs/export to team/mirsal-export-architecture.md section 7), `.webm` / `.png` / `.webp`, plus `manifest.json` v1; `GET /api/packs/{id}/telegram.zip` is the same files renamed with `@stickers` instructions, `POST /api/packs/{id}/stickers/{sid}/move {to}` for one sticker), `/api/stickers/move {to, items}` (bulk, all or nothing) and `/api/stickers/delete`, `/api/cutout`, `/api/projects…` (video / GIF projects),
`/api/telegram…` (status, config, disconnect; the token is never returned), `GET /api/search?q=` (Postgres when the database is up, else files), `POST /api/packs/{id}/stickers/{sid}/replace {file: base64, name?, others?: [ids] | true, others_only?, undo?}` (a file edited outside the app: same id, name, emoji and particle links, the type follows the file — PNG/JPG/WebP static 512 px 512 KB, WebM animated 512 px 256 KB; the batch and this pack take it, copies in other packs are offered, the old file kept for one undo),
`/api/watch…` (the watch folders: list, remove to the trash, restore, purge), `GET /api/history` (every batch, newest edit first, a page at a time: each item carries `grid: [rows, cols]` and `cells: [{index, row, col, png, status, animated}]`, so a card draws the sheet's own 3x3 / 2x2 as it was cut).

**Retime to 3 s (Haitham, 2026-10-12).** A closed loop can end before 3 s (the clip ends on the frame that leads back to its start). Every export asks
first when any animation in it is shorter than 2.95 s: Download .zip (pack and batch) and Send to Telegram show a dialog, *Retime to 3 s* or *Keep
their length* (the Telegram one says Telegram plays a video sticker for up to 3 s), Export to collection shows the same choice as a field. Retime plays
the same frames over 3 s, a lower frame rate, nothing invented, the loop kept (`library.retime_webm`, or inside `light_webm` for the small zip), and
the manifest row records `retimed_from`; the stored animations never change. `GET /api/packs/{id}/short` lists a pack's short animations
(`{seconds: 3, short: [{id, name, emoji, seconds}]}`); a batch's come from its own `anim_metrics` (frames_out / fps). Query `?retime=1` on both
`export.zip` routes, `retime: true` in the Telegram and collection bodies.

**Health** (every dependency reports itself; nothing raises): `GET /api/health` (database with write-through counters, Redis engine, models, providers, storage, the job queue's mode and counts),
`/api/health/models`, `/api/health/storage`, `GET /api/vision`. **Metrics**: `GET /api/metrics` (owner only; `python -m mirsal metrics` prints it): from what is already on disk, the time from a batch's request to its first cut sticker (median, p90, max), the share approved of the stickers a human decided at G2 (stills) and G4 (animations), the share of batches that are a redo, batches with no sticker, and one line per batch (`flow/metrics.py`).
